Every safety net you've added, timeouts, retries, even autoscaling, has the possibility to push you further over the edge instead of pulling you back. With AI, this cliff gets steeper. Then, when you add buffers, caches, and a backlog of work, the memory pressure builds up fast. The runtime starts spending more time managing memory than doing actual work.
