📋 Project Description

Islamic knowledge management and educational platform that helps educational institutions and Islamic centers deliver Quranic studies, manage religious content, and coordinate community activities. Built with ASP.NET, VB.NET, C#, this platform provides a complete solution for organizations seeking to modernize their operations. The system integrates multiple capabilities into a unified interface, reducing complexity and improving operational efficiency. Designed for both technical and non-technical users, it bridges the gap between organizational needs and technological capabilities.
