# CORTEX Documentation Positioning Guidelines

**Created:** December 10, 2025  
**Purpose:** Define realistic maturity-level positioning for CORTEX 3.0 vs 4.0  
**Status:** ACTIVE - Must be followed by all documentation generators

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary

**CRITICAL POSITIONING CHANGE:**
- **CORTEX 3.0:** Team-level tool (2-10 developers, local/small team deployment)
- **CORTEX 4.0:** Organization-level platform (multi-team, enterprise deployment - ASPIRATIONAL)

**Problem:** Current documentation overstates CORTEX 3.0 as "enterprise-grade" when reality is team-level maturity.

**Solution:** Recalibrate all content to reflect honest positioning while preserving aspirational 4.0 vision.

## 📋 Page-Specific Tone Guidelines

### Landing Page (`index.html`)
**BEFORE (Overstated):**
> "CORTEX 3.0 - Enterprise AI Development Platform"

**AFTER (Realistic):**
> "CORTEX 3.0 - Team-Level AI Development Assistant"

**Required Disclaimer:**
> "CORTEX 3.0 is optimized for teams of 2-10 developers with local or small-scale deployment. See CORTEX 4.0 Vision for our organization-scale roadmap."

### Feature Pages
**Pattern:** Emphasize team-level benefits, avoid "enterprise" language

**TDD Mastery:**
- ✅ "94% test-first success rate in team environments"
- ❌ "Enterprise TDD enforcement platform"

**Planning System 2.0:**
- ✅ "Team planning with DoR/DoD compliance"
- ❌ "Enterprise portfolio management system"

**Dashboard System:**
- ✅ "Team metrics visualization with real-time updates"
- ❌ "Executive analytics platform for C-suite"

### Architecture Pages
**Pattern:** Honest about current deployment scale, future scalability

**Four-Tier Brain:**
- ✅ "Tested in team environments (2-10 developers)"
- ✅ "4.0 Evolution: Distributed architecture for organization-scale"
- ❌ "Production-hardened for Fortune 500 companies"

**SKULL Protection:**
- ✅ "22 rules enforced in team workflows"
- ✅ "Validated in 100+ team development sessions"
- ❌ "Enterprise governance framework"

### Future Vision Pages
**Pattern:** Clearly distinguish 3.0 reality vs 4.0 aspiration

**Required Banner on `future/index.html`:**
> "🚀 CORTEX 4.0 = Organization-Scale Evolution (2025-2026 Roadmap)  
> These features build upon CORTEX 3.0's team-level foundation to enable organization-wide deployment."

**Strategic Goals:**
- Each goal must have "4.0 Target" badge
- Each goal must state: "Builds on 3.0 team-level foundation"

**Technical Evolution:**
- "3.0 Reality: Team-level tool (2-10 devs, local deployment)"
- "4.0 Vision: Organization-scale platform (multi-team, cloud-native)"

### Story Page (`story/index.html`)
**Added Context:**
> "This story documents the evolution of a team-level AI assistant with organization-scale ambitions. What started as a basement project for small teams is being architected for enterprise deployment in CORTEX 4.0."

**Chapter Endings (New Pattern):**
- **What We Built (3.0 - Team Level):** [actual implementation]
- **What's Next (4.0 - Organization Scale):** [future vision]

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Reducing Real Achievements
**DON'T:**
> "CORTEX only works for toy projects"

**DO:**
> "CORTEX 3.0 is production-ready for teams of 2-10 developers with proven success in real-world projects"

### Mistake 2: Overselling Current Capabilities
**DON'T:**
> "CORTEX replaces Jira, GitHub, and your entire dev stack"

**DO:**
> "CORTEX enhances your existing tools with AI-powered assistance for small teams"

### Mistake 3: Vague Future Promises
**DON'T:**
> "CORTEX will support enterprise features soon"

**DO:**
> "CORTEX 4.0 (Q1-Q4 2025 roadmap) will introduce organization-scale features including SSO, RBAC, and cloud-native deployment"

### Mistake 4: Ignoring Current Users
**DON'T:**
> "CORTEX 3.0 is just a prototype"

**DO:**
> "CORTEX 3.0 is actively used by development teams worldwide with 94% test-first success rate and continuous feature delivery"
