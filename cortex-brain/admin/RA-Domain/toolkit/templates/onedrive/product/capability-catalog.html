📖 Executive Summary - What This Application Does

The RA Domain is a .NET-based platform designed to manage healthcare benefits accounts—including FSAs, HRAs, and HSAs—for employers and their employees. It processes over 50,000 new accounts annually, orchestrates year-end carryover operations (handling 26,000 accounts in ~15-20 minutes), and maintains multi-year historical balance records for compliance reporting.

This catalog details 14 core business capabilities spanning account lifecycle, claims processing, regulatory compliance, and financial ledger management. Each capability is powered by domain services built with C#/.NET, Entity Framework, and NServiceBus messaging.

🔑 14 Key Business Capabilities

1️⃣ Account Management

The system creates, updates, and maintains healthcare benefit accounts tied to employer-sponsored insurance plans. Each account tracks an account holder, their dependents, and their plan enrollment history. Account records span multiple years and support mid-year changes, terminations, and reinstatements. Core services include AccountDomainService (450 LOC, 8 methods) and multi-tenant isolation to ensure data privacy.

2️⃣ Claims Processing (Highest Volume)

Claims flow from external systems via NServiceBus and are validated against account balances, IRS eligibility rules, and plan-specific limits. The ClaimDomainService (620 LOC, 12 methods) handles approvals, denials, and partial payments, while maintaining a full audit trail for HIPAA compliance. The system processes thousands of claims daily, with real-time balance updates.

3️⃣ Balance Management (3 Balance Types)

Every account tracks three balance types: Available (current spendable funds), Beginning (year-start balance), and Ending (year-end balance post-forfeiture). The BalanceDomainService (410 LOC) ensures real-time accuracy across claims, contributions, and carryovers, supporting both employer contributions and employee salary deferrals.

4️⃣ Year-End Carryover Processing

At the end of each plan year, accounts roll over unused balances according to IRS rules (FSA: $610 max, HSA: unlimited). The CarryoverDollarsDomainService (717 LOC, 15 methods) orchestrates this process, applying plan-specific rules, grace periods, and run-out periods.

V2 Batch Architecture: A refactored batch process (introduced in 2023) reduced execution time by 85%, processing 26,000 accounts in 15-20 minutes (vs. 90+ minutes in V1). It uses 10 concurrent workers, 1,000-account batches, and optimistic concurrency to prevent data corruption.

5️⃣ Card Transaction Processing

Members use debit cards for point-of-sale healthcare purchases. The system validates transactions in real-time against account balances and merchant category codes (MCCs). All card data is tokenized for PCI-DSS compliance, with authorization events logged for audit purposes. The CardTransactionService integrates with external payment processors via secure APIs.

6️⃣ Statements Generation (Monthly PDF)

The system generates monthly PDF statements showing account activity: contributions, claims, card transactions, and balances. Statements are archived for 7 years to meet ERISA record-keeping requirements. The StatementService (320 LOC) uses Entity Framework queries to aggregate transaction data.

⚠️ Known Issue: Statement generation fails for accounts opened before January 2025 due to a date-handling bug in the PDF renderer. This affects ~800 legacy accounts and is tracked as a P1 defect.

7️⃣ Forfeiture Management (Use-It-Or-Lose-It)

FSA accounts follow IRS "use-it-or-lose-it" rules: unused funds above the carryover limit ($610 for 2024) are forfeited to the employer. The ForfeitureService (280 LOC) calculates forfeitures at year-end, applies grace periods, and transfers funds to employer accounts. Forfeiture events are logged for financial audits.

8️⃣ Contribution Limit Validation (IRS Caps)

The system enforces IRS annual contribution limits: $3,200 for FSAs (2024), $4,150 for HSAs (individual), and $8,300 for HSAs (family). The ContributionLimitService (240 LOC) validates employer and employee contributions against these caps, preventing over-contributions that trigger tax penalties. Limits are configurable per plan year.

9️⃣ Ledger Management (PercentPlanLedger)

The PercentPlanLedgerDomainService (490 LOC) tracks employer contributions for percentage-based plans (e.g., employer pays 80% of premiums). It maintains a double-entry ledger for each account, recording debits, credits, and running balances. Ledger entries are immutable and support month-end reconciliation reports.

🔟 FlexPlan Processing

FlexPlans bundle multiple account types (FSA + HRA + HSA) under a single employer contract. The MemberFlexSpanDomainService (1,205 LOC, 22 methods) manages these bundles, coordinating enrollments, terminations, and year-end rollovers across account types. This is the largest domain service in the codebase and handles complex multi-account scenarios.

1️⃣1️⃣ Rollover Settings Configuration

Employers configure rollover rules at the plan level: maximum carryover amounts, grace periods, and run-out periods. The RolloverSettingsService (310 LOC) stores these rules and applies them during year-end processing. Settings are versioned to support mid-year plan amendments without impacting prior-year data.

1️⃣2️⃣ Global Contribution Limits

IRS limits change annually and must be updated in the system before each plan year. The GlobalContributionLimitService (180 LOC) maintains a reference table of IRS limits by year and account type. These values are used by the contribution validation engine and displayed in member portals.

1️⃣3️⃣ Compliance Audit Trail (7-Year Retention)

All account changes, balance adjustments, and administrative actions are logged to an audit table with user identity, timestamp, and reason codes. The AuditTrailService (220 LOC) ensures HIPAA-compliant logging (§164.312) and ERISA 7-year retention. Audit logs are immutable and support regulatory inquiries.

1️⃣4️⃣ Event-Driven Integration (NServiceBus)

The system publishes and subscribes to domain events via NServiceBus: AccountCreatedEvent, ClaimApprovedEvent, CarryoverCompletedEvent, and 20+ others. External systems (enrollment, billing, reporting) consume these events for downstream processing. The event-driven architecture decouples the RA Domain from legacy monoliths and supports gradual modernization.

✅ Architecture Highlights: Domain-Driven Design (DDD) with 11 domain services, Entity Framework Core for data access, NServiceBus for messaging, LaunchDarkly for feature flags, and multi-tenant data isolation. The codebase spans 256 files with 30+ domain entities.
