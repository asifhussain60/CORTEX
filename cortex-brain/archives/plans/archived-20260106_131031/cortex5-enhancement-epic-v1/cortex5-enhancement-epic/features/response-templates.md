# Feature: Response Templates & Accessibility
**F005** | 🟡 MEDIUM | Phase 4

## Purpose
WCAG AA-compliant autonomous execution templates with progress bars, concise mode default, accessibility rules.

## Requirements
- autonomous_execution_progress template
- Phase-level updates (not per-task)
- Max 40-line summaries
- No verbose narration ("Now I'll...", "Perfect!")
- COGNITIVE_LOAD, SILENT_TASKS, CONCISE_DEFAULT rules
