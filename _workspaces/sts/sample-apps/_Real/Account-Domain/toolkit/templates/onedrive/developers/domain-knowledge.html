🏥 What Is This Application?

The Payment Accounts platform is GenericCorp's enterprise system for managing tax-advantaged healthcare spending accounts on behalf of employers and their employees. Operating in the highly regulated healthcare benefits administration industry, it serves as the financial engine that processes medical expense reimbursements, tracks account balances, and ensures compliance with federal tax regulations.

At its core, the platform solves a complex regulatory challenge: enabling employees to set aside pre-tax dollars for qualified medical expenses while ensuring strict adherence to RegulatoryAgency contribution limits, PrivacyRegulation privacy requirements, and PaymentSecurity payment card security standards.

The system manages four distinct account types—Flexible Spending Accounts (FlexAccount), Health Savings Accounts (HealthSavings), Health Payment Arrangements (HealthReimbursement), and DependentCare FlexAccount—each with unique regulatory rules and business logic.

👥 Who Uses This Platform?

1️⃣ Organizations (Plan Sponsors)

Organizations act as plan sponsors, configuring payment account offerings for their workforce. They define plan parameters such as contribution limits, rollover rules, and eligibility criteria.

Key Activities:

  • Generate compliance reports (RegulatoryAgency Form 5500)
  • Monitor account utilization rates
  • Track forfeited funds that return to the organization at year-end

Technical Note: The platform's multi-tenant architecture ensures complete data isolation between employers, protecting competitive business information and employee privacy.

2️⃣ Customers (Account Holders)

Customers are employees who contribute pre-tax dollars to their payment accounts and submit claims for qualified medical expenses. They interact with the platform primarily through customer portals and mobile applications.

Key Activities:

  • Check account balances (real-time)
  • Submit expense receipts for payment
  • Track payment status
  • Make informed healthcare purchasing decisions

Technical Challenge: The system must present real-time balance information accounting for pending claims, scheduled disbursements, and available funds—a critical feature for customer decision-making.

3️⃣ Administrators & Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)

Administrators and TPAs operate the system on behalf of employers, managing day-to-day operations and resolving complex scenarios.

Key Activities:

  • Review and approve claims
  • Resolve disputes (balance discrepancies, request denials)
  • Manage account lifecycle events (terminations, plan year rollovers)
  • Handle mid-year plan changes due to life events

Technical Requirement: Comprehensive audit trails to investigate discrepancies, demonstrate regulatory compliance during external audits, and provide customer service support.

🏗️ Architectural Patterns You'll See

Domain-Driven Design (DDD)

The platform uses 11 domain services (ExampleDomainService, ClaimDomainService, BalanceDomainService, etc.) to encapsulate complex business rules. Each service represents a core business capability with well-defined inputs, outputs, and state transitions.

Example: ExampleDomainService (717 LOC, 15 methods) orchestrates year-end balance rollovers, applying RegulatoryAgency rollover limits, grace periods, and expiration rules.

Event-Driven Integration (NServiceBus)

When account balances change, the system publishes BalanceChangedEvent messages to a message queue. External systems (statement generation, reporting analytics, TPA synchronization) subscribe to these events asynchronously without blocking primary workflows.

Why This Matters: Downstream system failures (statement generation unavailable) do not block core platform workflows. Events queue for processing when systems recover, ensuring eventual consistency.

Multi-Tenant Isolation

The Organization entity serves as the tenant root, ensuring complete data segregation between client organizations. All queries filter by organization identifier, preventing accidental data leakage across organizational boundaries.

Developer Responsibility: Every database query MUST include WHERE employerId = @tenantId. This is enforced by code review and integration tests. Violating multi-tenancy is a P0 security issue.

⚠️ Regulatory Context: Everything you build must comply with RegulatoryAgency Publication 969, PrivacyRegulation §164.312, PaymentSecurity v4.0, and BenefitsRegulation disclosure requirements. The compliance-overview dashboard details these regulations. When in doubt, consult the compliance team before merging code.
