## Style Guidelines for All Images

**Aesthetic Combination:**
- Technical sci-fi cyberpunk meets warm, hopeful fantasy
- Glowing neon elements balanced with warm basement lighting
- Serious technical accuracy rendered in fantastical visual metaphors
- Basement setting grounded in reality but expanded into digital space

**Color Palette:**
- Warm: Amber, gold, warm white (basement lighting, achievement moments)
- Cool: Electric blue, cyan, purple (code, data flows, technical elements)
- Accents: Red (TIER-0 rules, errors), Green (passing tests, success), Orange (in-progress)

**Lighting:**
- Basement overhead lights creating practical shadows
- Glow effects from monitors and holographic displays
- Mystical/magical light effects for abstract concepts
- Sunrise/dawn lighting for hopeful moments

**Characters:**
- **Asif Codenstien**: Bloodshot eyes, faded hoodie, messy hair, determined expression
- **Miss Governance**: Professional appearance, severe but fair expression, appears younger than expected, carries a clipboard
- Both appear as thoughtful engineers, not stereotypical "nerds"

**Recurring Elements:**
- The temperamental Wi-Fi router (always slightly ominous, always present)
- Wobbly basement chair (symbol of humble beginnings)
- Glowing code on screens
- YAML configuration files
- Test results and progress indicators
- Circuit diagrams and orchestration patterns
