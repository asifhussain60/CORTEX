# Operations Guide

Operate CORTEX in production: monitoring, compliance, troubleshooting, and disaster recovery.

## Operational Responsibilities

### Monitoring & Alerting
Track system health, performance, and anomalies.

### Audit & Compliance
Maintain audit trails, verify governance compliance, handle regulatory requirements (GDPR/HIPAA/SOC2).

### Troubleshooting
Diagnose and resolve operational issues.

### Disaster Recovery
Backup and restore procedures, business continuity planning.

## Core Topics

- **Monitoring**: Application Insights integration, dashboard setup
- **Alerting**: Alert configuration, escalation procedures
- **Logging**: Log aggregation, analysis, retention
- **Compliance**: Audit procedures, regulatory mappings
