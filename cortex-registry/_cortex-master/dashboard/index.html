The CORTEX Story

CORTEX began as a vision: What if AI development could be intelligent, self-documenting, and continuously learning? Through 6 distinct eras and 23 phases, we've built a system that doesn't just execute code — it understands, validates, and evolves.

🧠

Intelligence First

LENS protocol sees your code structure, Git history, and architecture patterns

🛡️

Governance Built-In

4-tier brain validates every operation before, during, and after execution

📊

Observable Always

Prometheus metrics, audit trails, and beautiful dashboards tell the full story

Why CORTEX?

For Developers

Stop fighting tooling. CORTEX handles TDD enforcement, refactoring, documentation, and security scanning — you focus on business logic.

For Architects

Coherence validators ensure cross-layer consistency. Challenge engine forces justification. ARCH-003 compliance built-in.

For Organizations

Every decision traced, every change audited, every pattern learned. Scale with confidence knowing your AI development is governable.

For the Future

Self-learning from production, continuous improvement, and enterprise-grade reliability. CORTEX evolves with every deployment.
