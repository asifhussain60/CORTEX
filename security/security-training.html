Phishing Simulation Exercise

Can you spot the red flags? Analyze this email and decide if it's safe or a phishing attempt.

From: IT Security <security@cortex-support.net>
To: you@company.com
Subject: URGENT: Password Expiration Notice - Action Required

Dear Valued Employee,

Your corporate password will expire in 24 hours. To avoid account lockout, please update your credentials immediately by clicking the link below:

https://cortex-security-update.com/reset-password

Failure to update your password will result in immediate account suspension and loss of access to all company systems.

Best regards,
IT Security Team

Suspicious sender domain: The email claims to be from IT Security but uses "cortex-support.net" instead of your company's official domain.
Urgency and threats: Creating urgency with "24 hours" and threatening "immediate account suspension" are common phishing tactics.
Suspicious link: The URL "cortex-security-update.com" is not your company's official domain.
