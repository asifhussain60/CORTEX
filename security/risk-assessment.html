  • FAIR: Factor Analysis of Information Risk

Risk Treatment Options

Mitigate

Implement controls to reduce risk likelihood or impact. Most common treatment for significant risks.

Accept

Acknowledge the risk and its potential impact. Appropriate for low risks or when treatment cost exceeds benefit.

Transfer

Share risk with third parties through insurance, outsourcing, or contracts. Common for financial impacts.

Avoid

Eliminate the risk by removing the source. May involve changing processes or discontinuing activities.

Risk Assessment Best Practices

Regular Reviews

Conduct risk assessments quarterly or after significant changes. Continuous monitoring ensures risks are identified and addressed promptly.

Stakeholder Involvement

Include business owners, IT, security, and compliance teams. Different perspectives improve risk identification and treatment selection.

Business Context

Align risk assessments with business objectives. Consider impact on revenue, reputation, and strategic goals beyond technical severity.

Documentation

Maintain detailed records of assessments, decisions, and rationale. Documentation supports audits and provides institutional knowledge.

Risk Appetite

Define organizational risk appetite and tolerance levels. Use these thresholds to guide treatment decisions and resource allocation.

Control Mapping

Link risks to existing controls and compliance requirements. This ensures efficient resource use and identifies control gaps.
