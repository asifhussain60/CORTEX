What is "Sharpen The Saw"?

"Sharpen the Saw means preserving and enhancing the greatest asset you have—you." — Stephen Covey, The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People

For CORTEX, we applied this principle by creating a deliberately flawed codebase with 40+ documented anti-patterns across security, architecture, and code quality. Then we demonstrated CORTEX's capabilities by fixing every single one—with real before/after code.
