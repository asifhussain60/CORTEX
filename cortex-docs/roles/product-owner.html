20+ Workflow templates · 3-tier hierarchy

5 categories of PO rework eliminated by design

Vague acceptance criteria — DoR enforcement returns stories to the PO before any developer time is spent. Sprint scope creep — CORE-064 sweep completeness means every instance of a problem is fixed in one sprint, not spread across three. Security stories post-ship — LENS scans for CVEs and PHI/PII exposure at story level, before the sprint starts. Recurring defects — RCA Engine writes prevention rules to the governance registry; the same root cause cannot recur silently. Retrospective audit overhead — 32 CORE rules enforced automatically, with a queryable SQLite audit trail, eliminating compliance archaeology at sprint review.
