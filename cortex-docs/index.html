Did you know what Dogfooding means?

We thought you might ask. Here is the short version — and the proof.

Definition

Dogfooding

Dogfooding — short for "eating your own dog food" — means using your own product to build itself. If a tool is good enough to sell to others, it should be good enough for its creators to rely on every single day. It is the ultimate confidence test: you are not just claiming it works — you are proving it under the most demanding conditions possible.

💡

Classic example: Microsoft's Windows team famously ran their own unreleased builds of Windows as their daily OS. Every crash they hit, their users would never see. That is dogfooding in action.
