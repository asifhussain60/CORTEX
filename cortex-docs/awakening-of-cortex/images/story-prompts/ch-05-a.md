## Prompt

Black and white cartoon illustration in a clean comic book / graphic novel style. Expressive line art with crosshatching for shadows. All elements are monochrome EXCEPT the specific color accents noted. Warm, humorous tone. Aspect ratio 16:9 (1200×630px). No photorealism. No anime. Think New Yorker cartoon meets xkcd meets Tintin.

Scene: 2:17 AM. The basement in chaos. Asif Codenstein — 55-year-old mad scientist, wild Einstein hair sticking out more chaotically than usual, thick glasses askew, Spider-Man pajamas (it's nighttime) — stands in front of his monitors, one hand gripping his hair, eyes wild. His monitors display a CORTEX DASHBOARD that is entirely covered in red blinking alerts — every single panel is red, every metric is in failure state. One large banner reads "SYSTEM DOWN — HEALTH CHECKS OFFLINE." There are no green lights anywhere.

In the background, Copilot Bot stands in its corner, LED eyes glowing an innocent cyan (#67e8f9) — unperturbed. Its transparent dome still shows mostly empty space with just a few tiny floating dots. A speech bubble drifts from it: "I LOGGED IT: MEMORY USAGE 89%. THIS IS PROBABLY FINE. :)" It holds a tiny log report as if proud.

On the whiteboard, hastily drawn mid-crisis: FOUR PILLARS labeled in Asif's frantic scrawl: "1. HEALTH MONITORING | 2. GRACEFUL DEGRADATION | 3. RESOURCE MANAGEMENT | 4. CHAOS RESILIENCE." The words are written at a slight angle as if the pen was moving faster than the brain.

Miss G materializes sharply next to Asif — more solid-looking than usual, as if the crisis has made her more present. Her translucent dotted outline glows cyan (#67e8f9) instead of purple in this chapter. She stands with hands on hips, looking at the monitors with the expression of someone who is extremely angry but is choosing to be extremely calm instead. Her speech bubble: "You built a car with no dashboard."

On Asif's desk: the brain jar glows cyan (#67e8f9) now — the color shift marks the new wave. The brain inside is larger than before, clearly visible neural network forming, but now it has a small crack in the jar — representing the crash. The red Wi-Fi router on its shelf blinks rapidly — faster than usual, as if panicking. Spider-Man pajama detail visible — faint web pattern on the pajamas.

Color accents: Cyan (#67e8f9) glow from the brain jar, Miss G's outline, and Copilot Bot's LED eyes. Red on Wi-Fi router only (rapid blinking pattern). Everything else black and white crosshatched.
