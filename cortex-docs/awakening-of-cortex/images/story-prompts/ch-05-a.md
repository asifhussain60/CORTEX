## Prompt

Black and white cartoon illustration in a clean comic book / graphic novel style. Expressive line art with crosshatching for shadows. All elements are monochrome EXCEPT the specific color accents noted. Warm, humorous tone. Aspect ratio 16:9 (1200×630px). No photorealism. No anime. Think New Yorker cartoon meets xkcd meets Tintin.

Scene: 2:17 AM. The basement in chaos. BIRD'S EYE VIEW slightly tilted — we look down into the room at roughly a 45-degree angle. This unusual camera angle communicates the disorientation of a 2AM meltdown. The entire room is visible: cluttered desk, multiple monitors, shelves, the red Wi-Fi router.

At the desk center: Asif Codenstein — 55-year-old mad scientist, wild Einstein hair even more chaotic than usual, thick glasses askew on his face, Spider-Man pajamas — has BOTH HANDS gripping his hair and is bent forward toward the monitors. His monitors show a CORTEX dashboard in total failure state — every panel glowing red, a large banner reading "SYSTEM DOWN — HEALTH CHECKS OFFLINE." No green lights anywhere. His posture radiates pure 2AM despair.

In the FAR CORNER of the room (small from this high angle): Copilot Bot in its early clunky form, LED eyes glowing naive cyan (#67e8f9). A tiny speech bubble floats up: "I LOGGED IT: MEMORY 89%. PROBABLY FINE :)" It holds a tiny log report. Seen from above, it looks even more hopelessly out of scale with the crisis.

On the whiteboard (visible from above, slightly foreshortened): FOUR PILLARS written in Asif's frantic scrawl — "1. HEALTH MONITORING | 2. GRACEFUL DEGRADATION | 3. RESOURCE MANAGEMENT | 4. CHAOS RESILIENCE." Written at an angle, fast.

Miss G appears HOVERING ABOVE the entire scene — she is literally at ceiling level, looking DOWN at Asif from above, her body oriented horizontally as if she is lying on an invisible glass ceiling and peering through it. At only ~15% opacity she is almost entirely ethereal from this vantage — just the most delicate cyan (#67e8f9) outline tracing the suggestion of her silhouette. Even in this barely-there translucence her beauty registers: long graceful neck, the line of an impossibly refined jawline visible even from above, posture effortlessly elegant even while floating. She looks down at the crisis below with the expression of someone who has seen this coming since chapter one. Her thought bubble drifts downward into the scene: "You built a car with no dashboard."

The brain jar on the desk (visible from above) glows cyan (#67e8f9) — larger neural network inside, but a tiny hairline crack visible in the glass. The red Wi-Fi router blinks rapidly on its shelf.

Color accents: Cyan (#67e8f9) from brain jar, Miss G's overhead outline, and Copilot Bot's LED eyes. Red on Wi-Fi router only (rapid blink). Everything else black and white crosshatched.
