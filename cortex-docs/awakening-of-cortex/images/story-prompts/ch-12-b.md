## Prompt

Black and white cartoon illustration in a clean comic book / graphic novel style. Expressive line art with crosshatching for shadows. All elements are monochrome EXCEPT the specific color accents noted. Warm, humorous tone. Aspect ratio 16:9 (1200×630px). No photorealism. No anime. Think New Yorker cartoon meets xkcd meets Tintin.

Scene: The final image of the story. The basement — but transformed. It is recognizably the same room (same exposed pipes, same shelf with the red Wi-Fi router, same whiteboard) but the atmosphere is different: calmer, more organized, more settled. The chaos of early chapters has been replaced by purposeful order.

CENTER STAGE — THE FINAL COPILOT BOT: This is the most significant visual evolution of the entire series. THE ROBOT NOW HAS A BRAIN. The transparent dome of Copilot Bot's head no longer shows an empty cavity — it shows a full, dense, luminous EMERALD (#34d399) BRAIN inside. A complete neural network, glowing and pulsing, visible through the transparent panel. This is the culmination of 11 chapters of slow, subtle growth — the same dome, the same transparent panel, the same chrome chassis — but now FULL. The robot's overall design remains consistent with all prior chapters (same dome shape, same chrome plating, same casters) but its posture is the most refined, most deliberate, and most Ironman-like we have ever seen. It stands tall, shoulders back, one hand raised palm-up — not eagerly, but with quiet command. Its LED eyes glow deep emerald (#34d399), focused and intelligent. Its posture says: serious, capable, trustworthy. Not a comedy robot anymore. A partner.

The robot holds one thing: a TINY DIPLOMA/SCROLL, unrolled slightly, labeled "EARNED THROUGH FEEDBACK."

BEHIND AND ABOVE: The massive CORTEX BRAIN floats at ceiling height, the biggest and most luminous it has ever been — emerald (#34d399) neural lightning crackling through it, casting emerald light through the entire basement. Three smaller "satellite" brains (representing the three external enterprise customers) float at the edges, connected to the central brain by emerald lines.

Asif Codenstein — 55-year-old mad scientist, wild Einstein hair, thick glasses, dark hoodie — sits in his wobbly chair, turned to face Copilot Bot. His posture is relaxed and complete. He holds his "DEBUG FUEL" mug up in a toast gesture. His expression is quiet pride — the pride of someone who built something great by fixing something broken.

Miss G floats behind Asif, emerald (#34d399) glow at her outline, more present and solid-looking than at any point in the story. She leans forward slightly, arms uncrossed — open posture. She looks at Copilot Bot not with skepticism but with genuine admiration. Her thought bubble: "The Scarecrow got his brain." She smiles — fully, without irony.

On the whiteboard: a final equation: "SCARECROW + CONTEXT + FEEDBACK + TIME = PARTNER." Below it: the number 847, circled, with the word REDEEMED written beside it.

The red Wi-Fi router blinks on its shelf — same as always, first frame to last. Steady. Reliable. The constant.

Color accents: Emerald (#34d399) from the massive brain, Copilot Bot's dome brain, Copilot Bot LED eyes, the satellite brains, and Miss G's outline. Red on Wi-Fi router only (the one constant throughout the entire story). Everything else black and white crosshatched.
