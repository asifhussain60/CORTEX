## Prompt

Black and white cartoon illustration in a clean comic book / graphic novel style. Expressive line art with crosshatching for shadows. All elements are monochrome EXCEPT the specific color accents noted. Warm, humorous tone. Aspect ratio 16:9 (1200×630px). No photorealism. No anime. Think New Yorker cartoon meets xkcd meets Tintin.

Scene: A conceptual / slightly surreal basement scene. The room is the same familiar basement but the CORTEX system is visualized as a living organism. The free-floating brain (amber #fbbf24 glow, large and dense) hovers at the center. Around it, orbiting in circular paths, are EIGHT SMALL AGENT FIGURES — tiny cartoon robots or shield-icons, each labeled:

  GOVERNANCE • DEPENDENCY • PERFORMANCE • CONSISTENCY
  SECURITY • TEST HEALTH • ARCHITECTURE • AUDIT

Each tiny agent continuously patrols its orbit around the brain. They are small (thumb-sized compared to the main characters), busy, purposeful. They radiate amber (#fbbf24) glow as they move.

Asif Codenstein — 55-year-old mad scientist, wild Einstein hair, thick glasses, dark hoodie — sits back in his wobbly chair watching the orbiting agents with a deeply satisfied expression and his feet on the desk. He holds a cup of coffee toward the display as if toasting it. His posture is relaxed but alert — the posture of a founder watching their system work without them. One monitor shows: "8 AGENTS ACTIVE — 0 THREATS — UPTIME 99.97%."

Copilot Bot stands in the frame, its appearance now noticeably more refined compared to Chapter 01. Still chrome, still dome-headed, but proportions are more elegant — the head is appropriately sized, not oversized. The transparent dome interior shows a clear sparse network of amber-glowing nodes connected by thin lines — the first time the dome looks like it contains the beginning of something deliberate, not just random dots. LED eyes glow amber (#fbbf24), steady and focused. Its speech bubble: "CAN I BE BOTH THE PATIENT AND AN IMMUNE CELL?" It still has the same fundamental character design — this is evolution, not replacement.

Miss G floats ethereally to the right, amber (#fbbf24) glow at her outline. She watches the orbiting agents with real admiration. Her thought bubble: "Invisible. Like plumbing. Nobody thinks about plumbing until it breaks."

On the whiteboard: setup-mcp.py is labeled with platform logos (apple/penguin/windows) all connected to a single CORTEX arrow. The red Wi-Fi router blinks on its shelf. The system feels alive, breathing, self-maintaining.

Color accents: Amber (#fbbf24) from the brain, all 8 agent icons, Copilot Bot LEDs and dome nodes, and Miss G's outline. Red on Wi-Fi router only. Everything else black and white crosshatched.
