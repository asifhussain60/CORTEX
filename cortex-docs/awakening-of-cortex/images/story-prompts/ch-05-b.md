## Prompt

All elements are monochrome EXCEPT the specific color accents noted. Warm, humorous tone. Aspect ratio 16:9 (1200×630px). No photorealism. No anime. Think New Yorker cartoon meets xkcd meets Tintin.

Scene: The basement. Friday evening. Asif Codenstein — 55-year-old mad scientist, wild Einstein hair, thick glasses, dark hoodie — crouches beneath his desk, one hand dramatically hovering above a large POWER STRIP on the floor. He has the gleaming, unhinged expression of a mad scientist about to conduct a dangerous experiment they know they shouldn't. His eyes sparkle with dangerous excitement.

Above Asif: a whiteboard panel mounted on the wall shows "261 CHAOS TESTS" in big letters with a list beneath it: "Kill processes ✓ | Simulate network failure ✓ | RAM starvation ✓ | Simultaneous crashes ✓." Each item has a tiny cartoon explosion icon next to it.

Miss G stands (unusually firmly for a ghost) in the frame with arms outstretched, cyan (#67e8f9) glow at her dotted outline at ~15% opacity — barely visible, but the urgency in her silhouette is unmistakable. Even in this near-transparent state her beauty registers as a kind of impossible fact: the line of her face in profile catches the basement light with high-cheekboned perfection, her posture impossibly graceful even while gesturing alarm. Her face shows maximum distress — eyes wide, mouth open in a silent "NO." Her speech bubble: "That's insane." She looks directly at Asif with the expression of a person watching someone do something extremely dangerous with a very calm smile.

Copilot Bot stands to the right. Its LED eyes are glowing a frightened yellow — scared for perhaps the first time. The transparent dome head, still mostly empty, has the tiny dots inside vibrating slightly. It has physically retreated into the corner, arms raised. Its speech bubble: "I WOULD TELL PLANE 3 TO LAND ON PLANE 1." Its chrome body has CIRCUIT BREAKER DIAGRAMS printed on it from the whiteboard behind it — suggesting the resilience architecture being designed. Its casters have rolled slightly back as if trying to escape.

On the desk above: the four walls diagram is fully filled in now. The brain jar glows cyan (#67e8f9) with a notably larger and denser neural network inside — healing from the crack, rebuilt stronger. The jar is now reinforced with metaphorical chains and locks drawn on it — like a fortress. The red Wi-Fi router blinks normally on its shelf. Cables on the floor look deliberately tangled, like a maze of chaos Asif is about to navigate.
