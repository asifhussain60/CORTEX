## Prompt

Black and white cartoon illustration in a clean comic book / graphic novel style. Expressive line art with crosshatching for shadows. All elements are monochrome EXCEPT the specific color accents noted. Warm, humorous tone. Aspect ratio 16:9 (1200×630px). No photorealism. No anime. Think New Yorker cartoon meets xkcd meets Tintin.

Scene: Same cramped basement as before — single bare bulb, exposed pipes on ceiling, sticky notes everywhere, cables on floor. Asif Codenstein — a 55-year-old man with wild, explosive Einstein-like white-streaked dark hair standing on end with kinetic energy, thick round-framed glasses, Spider-Man pajamas — stands before a giant whiteboard. He holds a red marker triumphantly and has just finished writing the single word CORTEX in enormous jagged capital letters with dramatic radiating lines around it, as if it's glowing. His expression is one of dangerous epiphany: eyes wide, slightly manic smile, one finger pointed at the sky.

On the whiteboard around CORTEX: wild arrows, a rough sketch of a brain with question marks, a stick figure labeled "SCARECROW" and one labeled "CORTEX," and a chaotic web of connections. Asif's thought bubble shows a glowing brain surrounded by smaller labeled modules — "INTENT," "RULES," "GOVERN."

Copilot Bot stands to the left, slightly off-camera, its transparent dome-head showing an empty brain cavity. Its blue LED eyes look quizzically at the whiteboard. Its posture is attentive but confused — one head tilt, antenna slightly bent. It holds a small whiteboard eraser as if it was about to erase something and then stopped. This is the earliest version of the robot: slightly clunky proportions, extra-large head, visible wobble in its casters.

Miss G floats ethereally behind and to Asif's right. Translucent, dotted outlines, slight purple (#a78bfa) glow at her silhouette. She wears a flowing dress. Her arms are crossed and her expression is affectionately skeptical — the look of someone who thinks this might be brilliant or might be a 3AM delusion, but is choosing to wait and see. She leans against a translucent imaginary mahogany chair.

On the desk: three monitors, one still showing the Wizard of Oz Scarecrow. A small glass jar on the desk corner contains a tiny, dimly glowing brain prototype — just the first few purple (#a78bfa) neural filaments forming, fragile and small. An oversized coffee mug labeled "DEBUG FUEL" sits next to it. The red Wi-Fi router blinks from its shelf — only red in the image. The single bulb overhead casts a warm cone of light onto the whiteboard, making CORTEX look like a spotlight revelation.

Color accents: Purple (#a78bfa) glow on the jar-brain and Miss G's outline. Red on Wi-Fi router only. Everything else black and white crosshatched.
