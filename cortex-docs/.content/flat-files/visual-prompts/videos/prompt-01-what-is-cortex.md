# Video Prompt 01 — What Is CORTEX?

> **Duration:** 7 minutes · **Audience:** Everyone — executives, engineers, curious learners
> **Depth:** 🟢 High-level overview — no code, no jargon
> **Purpose:** Inspire the viewer to watch all 8 concept videos and explore the tutorials
> **No overlap:** Image prompts show static architecture anatomy; this video tells the *story* of why CORTEX exists and what it does for you

---

## ⚠️ VISUAL IDENTITY — MANDATORY

> See `README.md` in this folder for the full mandatory palette, motion style, text contrast rules, typography, and watermark specification. All concept videos use the **cyan/purple glassmorphism** theme.

## ⚠️ NARRATION RULE — MANDATORY

> **The narrator never reads the slide.** Every narration line must add something the viewer cannot get from reading the screen: the *why*, the *consequence*, the *non-obvious implication*, or the *emotional truth*. If a narration line restates visible text, cut it or rewrite it. See `README.md` §Narration Philosophy for full guidance and examples.

---

## PROMPT

Create a 7-minute animated explainer video titled **"What Is CORTEX?"** using the glassmorphism visual identity.

### Scene 1 — The Problem (0:00 – 1:30)

**Open on:** A developer's screen — glassmorphic dark IDE panels. Show the chaos of modern AI-assisted development:

- A chat window produces an answer, but the developer manually copies it, edits a file, runs tests, checks governance rules, updates docs. Each manual step is a separate floating glass panel that blinks amber.
- Count the steps: glass panels stack up, wobble, some turn red — missed test, broken rule, forgotten doc update.
- The stack collapses in a gentle cascade — cyan particles scatter.

**Narration:** "The frustration isn't incompetence. It's overhead. Every minute spent on process is a minute not spent on the problem you were actually hired to solve."

### Scene 2 — Enter CORTEX (1:30 – 3:00)

**Transition:** Collapsed panels reassemble — a glowing cyan ring emerges from the center, pulling them into orbit.

- The ring is CORTEX. Text materializes: **"CO**gnitive **R**eal-**T**ime **EX**ecution" in Space Grotesk.
- The ring transforms into a translucent glass brain. Three tiers light up:
  - **Perception:** Cyan — "Understands what you're asking"
  - **Reasoning:** Purple — "Figures out the best approach"
  - **Action:** Green — "Executes with precision and tests"
- Analogy overlay on dark pill: *"CORTEX is the restaurant manager who coordinates the chef, waiter, and inspector — so they work in harmony."*

**Narration:** "Most frameworks make you adopt their way of working. CORTEX orchestrates the AI you already use — it doesn't replace your tools, it gives them a conductor."

**CRITICAL — Honesty callout** (glassmorphic info card):
> "CORTEX doesn't contain its own AI models. It orchestrates your existing AI (GitHub Copilot, GPT) — structuring prompts, validating results, and enforcing quality."

### Scene 3 — The Core Capabilities (3:00 – 5:00)

**Camera slowly orbits the glass brain.** Five capabilities appear as glassmorphic cards:

1. **Orchestration** — Show luminous dots orbiting in colored rings. Card: "Specialized orchestrators, each with one job — routed by intent."
   - Analogy: *"Like a hospital with specialist doctors — the cardiologist never does brain surgery."*

2. **MCP Tools** — Glass toolbar with tool slots filling in. Card: "Plug-in tools that extend AI capabilities through an open standard."
   - Analogy: *"Swiss Army knife — each tool folds out when you need it."*

3. **Governance** — Translucent shield wall shimmers across the bottom. Card: "Automated quality guardrails, enforced at every commit."
   - Analogy: *"Building codes for your code — inspected before anything ships."*

4. **TDD-First** — ECG heartbeat pulse: red peak, green peak, blue peak. Card: "Test first. Always. No exceptions."
   - Analogy: *"Taste the dish before serving it to customers."*

5. **LENS Intelligence** — Scanning beam sweeps across a code panel. Card: "Workspace-aware analysis — your entire codebase, understood in under a second."
   - Analogy: *"An MRI scan for your codebase."*

### Scene 4 — What Makes It Different (5:00 – 6:15)

**Split screen.** Left: "Without CORTEX" (dim). Right: "With CORTEX" (vibrant).

| Without | With |
|---|---|
| Manual file editing | Autonomous execution |
| Hope-based testing | TDD-first guarantee |
| No governance | Tiered rules enforced automatically |
| Scattered tools | Unified MCP toolkit |
| One-shot prompts | Orchestrated intelligence pipeline |

Animate each row appearing sequentially. Left stays dim, right glows.

**Narration:** "Each of these capabilities exists because someone, somewhere, learned a painful lesson without it. CORTEX encodes those lessons so you don't have to repeat them."

### Scene 5 — Your Learning Journey (6:15 – 7:00)

**Glassmorphic roadmap** showing the 8-video journey + tutorials:

- Videos 1–3: "Understand the foundation" (you are here ←)
- Videos 4–6: "Master the engineering discipline"
- Videos 7–8: "Extend and transform"
- Tutorials: "Hands-on practice"

**Narration:** "This is the first of eight videos. They're designed to build on each other — not just to inform you, but to change how you think about what AI-assisted engineering can actually look like. Stay with it."

**Four persona cards** slide in:
1. 🏢 **Business Leader** — "See governance compliance at a glance"
2. 📋 **Product Owner** — "Track capabilities and engineering health"
3. 💻 **Software Engineer** — "TDD, orchestrators, MCP tools — your daily workflow"
4. 🎓 **Curious Learner** — "Understand AI engineering patterns"

**Closing text:** **"One framework. Your time back."**

Logo pulse. End card with CORTEX URL.

## Notes
- This is the ONLY video that uses the word "framework" — subsequent videos focus on capabilities, not labels
- The honesty callout about LLM orchestration (not embedded AI) is critical — prevents misconceptions
- The learning journey roadmap at the end is the hook that drives viewers to watch all 8 videos
- **No hardcoded counts** — capabilities described by function, not by number
