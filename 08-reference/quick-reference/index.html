    Key Governance Rules¶

    CORTEX enforces 29 immutable governance rules (SKULL framework) at tier 0. These govern: - ✅ Circuit breaker patterns (CORE-001, CORE-002) - ✅ State consistency (CORE-003, CORE-004, CORE-005) - ✅ Concurrency control (CORE-006 through CORE-010) - ✅ Observability (CORE-011 through CORE-015) - ✅ Security (CORE-016 through CORE-029)

    See governance-rules.md for complete reference.
