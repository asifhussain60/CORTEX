## Quick Checklist (per video)

1. ☐ Open Google NotebookLM → **New notebook**
2. ☐ Upload the **source file** + all listed **content files** as sources
3. ☐ Paste the **steering prompt** into the NotebookLM chat to kick off generation
4. ☐ Do **not** upload the steering prompt as a source — it is an instruction, not content
