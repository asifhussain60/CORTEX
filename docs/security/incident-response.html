Incident Severity Classification

P1 Critical
Response: Immediate (< 15 min)
Resolution: 4 hours
Active breach, data exfiltration, ransomware, service outage
P2 High
Response: 1 hour
Resolution: 24 hours
Compromised credentials, malware detection, suspected intrusion
P3 Medium
Response: 4 hours
Resolution: 72 hours
Phishing attempt, policy violation, suspicious activity
P4 Low
Response: Next business day
Resolution: 1 week
Vulnerability disclosure, minor policy violation, audit finding
