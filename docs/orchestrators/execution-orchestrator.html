Execution Orchestrator

Autonomous plan execution with phase-by-phase validation gates, automatic rollback on failure, and clear intervention options. Your approved plan, executed perfectly.

Auto Execution 100% Phase Gates Rollback Support

Once you have a plan, you need to execute it—but manually tracking which phase you're on, running validations, and handling failures is tedious and error-prone. The Execution Orchestrator takes your approved plan and runs it autonomously, phase by phase, with validation gates ensuring each step completes successfully before moving forward. If something fails, it pauses and gives you clear options for rollback or manual intervention.
