CORTEX Orchestrators

6 USER-facing autonomous workflow coordinators

CORTEX orchestrators are intelligent workflow coordinators that transform natural language commands into complete, multi-phase operations. Instead of manually managing each step of feature planning, TDD enforcement, or code sanitization, you simply describe what you want— and the orchestrator handles the discovery, validation, execution, and verification automatically. Think of them as experienced team leads who know exactly how to break down complex tasks into manageable phases with quality gates at every step.

🏗️ How Orchestrators Work

Every CORTEX orchestrator follows a consistent pattern for reliability and predictability:

1 📋
Intent Parsing

Understand natural language command

2 🔍
Discovery

Analyze codebase and context

3 ✅
Validation

Check prerequisites (DoR)

4 ⚡
Execution

Perform multi-phase work

5 📊
Verification

Validate outcomes (DoD)
