# Character Reference Sheet - The Awakening of CORTEX

**Image Type:** Comic Story Illustration (NOT Technical Diagram)  
**Purpose:** Character reference for story page sidebar and consistency across all comic illustrations  
**Style:** Black and white cartoon, newspaper comic strip aesthetic  
**Format:** Three-panel character sheet

---

## DALL-E Prompt

```
Black and white cartoon illustration in newspaper comic strip style. Character reference sheet with three distinct panels arranged horizontally:

PANEL 1 - MR. CODENSTEIN (Einstein-style crazy professor):
- Wild, unkempt white hair sticking out in all directions (Einstein-inspired)
- Thick round glasses (slightly crooked)
- Lab coat with coffee stains and pen marks
- Disheveled appearance, stubble
- Hunched posture from too much computer work
- Expression: Manic enthusiasm mixed with exhaustion
- Small details: Pocket protector with too many pens, frayed sleeve cuffs

PANEL 2 - MISS G (Pretty ethereal apparition):
- Elegant, graceful figure (pretty but not overly detailed)
- Slightly translucent with subtle shimmer effect around edges
- Flowing hair suggesting movement/ethereal nature
- Arms crossed in signature pose
- One eyebrow raised (knowing expression)
- Simple dress or professional attire (business casual)
- Expression: Amused skepticism, wisdom with humor
- Ghostly effect: Light radiating lines, not fully solid

PANEL 3 - COPILOT (Friendly robot assistant):
- Rounded, friendly robot design (not threatening)
- Screen/monitor face showing emoji-style expressions
- Simple geometric body (circles, rectangles)
- Articulated arms (slight cartoon exaggeration)
- Question mark floating above head (confusion indicator)
- Expression: Evolving from confused to enlightened (show progression)
- Details: Antenna, gear symbols, binary code decoration

Overall style: Clean line art, high contrast black and white, humorous expressions, simple but expressive. Character designs should be consistent for use across all story illustrations. Think Calvin and Hobbes or Dilbert comic strip aesthetic - simple but personality-rich.
```
