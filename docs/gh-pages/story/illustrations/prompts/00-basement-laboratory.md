```

- Seventeen coffee mugs scattered across all surfaces (various states of decay)
- Large whiteboard on wall covered in frantic diagrams with arrows pointing everywhere
- Sticky notes forming rough neural network pattern on another wall

DETAILED CLUTTER:
- Folding chairs supporting networking equipment and routers
- Christmas decorations visible in background corner (relocated storage)
- Storage boxes labeled "Kitchen Stuff" acting as makeshift tables
- Cables and USB cords tangled across floor
- Empty pizza boxes stacked in corner
- Stack of technical books serving as monitor stand
- Mold growing in three visible coffee mugs (skull symbols for comedy)

WHITEBOARD CONTENT:
- Rough sketch of "TIER ARCHITECTURE" with scribbled layers
- Flow chart with too many arrows
- Coffee cup stain ring perfectly preserving earlier diagram
- "SKULL RULES" with actual skull doodle
- Question marks and exclamation points scattered around

LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE:
- Single desk lamp creating dramatic shadows
- Computer screen glow illuminating Mr. Codenstein's face
- Contrast showing both order (computer setup) and chaos (everything else)

Style: High contrast black and white, cross-hatching for shadows, simple line art with density showing clutter. Comic strip aesthetic like Pearls Before Swine or FoxTrot. Humorous details that reward close examination.
```
