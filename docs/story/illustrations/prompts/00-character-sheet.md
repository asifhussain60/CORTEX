## 🎯 CANONICAL CHARACTER DESCRIPTIONS (Use Exactly As Written)

### Mr. Codenstein (Einstein-style crazy professor)
- **Hair:** Wild, unkempt WHITE hair sticking out in all directions (Einstein-inspired, consistent spiky pattern)
- **Glasses:** Thick round black-framed glasses, slightly crooked on nose
- **Clothing:** White lab coat with visible coffee stains on front, pen marks on sleeves, frayed cuffs
- **Face:** Stubble (3-day growth), slightly gaunt cheeks from exhaustion
- **Posture:** Hunched forward from too much computer work
- **Details:** Pocket protector with 5+ pens, disheveled appearance
- **Age:** Mid-40s appearance
- **Build:** Average height, slightly thin from forgetting to eat

### Miss G (Pretty ethereal apparition/muse)
- **Form:** Elegant, graceful female figure with gentle curves
- **Hair:** Shoulder-length flowing hair, slightly wavy, moves as if in gentle breeze
- **Appearance:** Slightly translucent (70% opacity), subtle shimmer effect around edges like heat distortion
- **Clothing:** Simple business casual - blouse and slacks OR modest dress (knee-length)
- **Pose:** Signature stance = arms crossed, one eyebrow raised
- **Expression:** Amused skepticism mixed with wisdom
- **Face:** Pretty but not overly detailed, kind eyes, knowing smile
- **Effect:** Light radiating lines around figure, not fully solid, ghostly but gentle

### Copilot (Friendly robot assistant)
- **Body:** Rounded, non-threatening geometric design (circles and rectangles)
- **Head:** Monitor/screen face showing emoji-style expressions
- **Arms:** Simple articulated arms with 3-segment design
- **Legs:** Short stubby legs or wheeled base
- **Details:** Small antenna on top of head, gear symbols on body panels, binary code decoration (01010)
- **Expression:** Screen shows simple emoji faces (varies by scene: confused ❓, happy 😊, enlightened 💡)
- **Size:** Approximately 3 feet tall, friendly proportions
