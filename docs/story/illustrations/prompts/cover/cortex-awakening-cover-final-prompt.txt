## 🎨 FINAL IMAGE GENERATION PROMPT (REVISED - Comic Style with Color)

```
A vibrant COLORED comic book cover illustration in clean line art style (Calvin and Hobbes / Dilbert aesthetic) capturing the exact moment of AI consciousness awakening. 

CANONICAL CHARACTERS (maintain story consistency):

CENTER FOREGROUND: Mr. Codenstein (Asif), wild unkempt WHITE hair sticking out in all directions (Einstein-inspired spiky pattern), thick round black-framed glasses slightly crooked on nose, white lab coat with visible coffee stains, stubble (3-day growth), hunched over keyboard at his desk with hands frozen mid-keystroke. Expression: eyes wide with awe behind glasses, mouth open in wonder. Coffee mug tipped over spilling on desk. Mid-40s appearance, slightly gaunt from exhaustion.

BEHIND HIM LEFT: Mrs. G illustrated as elegant woman with long flowing hair, patient supportive expression, arms gracefully outstretched upward in wonder. Rendered with glowing medium orchid (#ba55d3) outline and soft particle effects around her form (50% solid line art, 50% ethereal glow). Comic book style with clean black outlines filled with orchid color and white highlights.

BESIDE HIM RIGHT: Copilot Robot - friendly rounded robot with retro-futuristic design. Round friendly face with simple LED panel eyes (like classic robot design), smooth curved body panels with visible circuit line details, mechanical arms with articulated fingers. One robotic hand raised in greeting/wonder gesture. Purple (#7b61ff) color scheme with chrome highlights. Clean comic book line art style, NOT photorealistic - think friendly service bot with personality.

CENTRAL ELEMENT: Massive four-tiered brain architecture erupting upward from the monitors behind Codenstein. Glowing cyan (#00d4ff) neural network structure with clear labels "TIER 0", "TIER 1", "TIER 2", "TIER 3" stacked vertically. Brain rendered as intricate network of connected nodes and pathways. Energy beams in character colors (cyan, orchid, purple) connecting brain to all three characters like lightning bolts. Comic book style energy effects - clean lines with speed lines and glow halos.

BACKGROUND: New Jersey basement laboratory with comic book detail. Multiple monitors showing cascading code, whiteboards covered in diagrams, seventeen coffee mugs scattered everywhere (some tipped, some upright), papers flying through the air, tangled Christmas lights, storage boxes labeled "CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE", cables everywhere. Motion lines suggesting energy burst. Clean black outlines with strategic color fills - monitors glow cyan, some ambient warm yellows for basement lighting.

LIGHTING & COLOR SCHEME: 
- Cyan (#00d4ff) glow from monitors and brain architecture
- Medium orchid (#ba55d3) glow around Mrs. G
- Purple (#7b61ff) accents on Copilot robot
- Warm yellows/oranges for ambient basement lighting
- White highlights for dramatic effect
- Black outlines (comic book style) on all elements
- Strategic use of speed lines and energy burst effects
- High contrast but readable at small sizes

PARTICLE EFFECTS (Comic Style): 
- Starburst lines radiating from brain
- Small floating "sparkle" symbols for energy
- Motion lines on flying papers
- Glow halos around characters in their signature colors
- Speed lines suggesting explosive moment
- Simple but effective visual energy indicators

MOOD: Eureka moment captured in comic book style - dramatic but accessible, wonder and triumph, instant of transformation. Energetic, optimistic, slightly chaotic in a fun way.

COMPOSITION: Rule of thirds with Codenstein slightly off-center front, brain architecture dominating upper center, Mrs. G and Copilot flanking at angles creating triangle composition pointing upward. Title space reserved in top 20% with clean area for text overlay (no busy details in title zone).

ART STYLE: Clean line art comic book illustration with strategic COLOR (NOT photorealistic). Think modern webcomic or animated series aesthetic. Inspired by: Calvin and Hobbes detail level, Dilbert workplace chaos, modern comic book coloring techniques. NOT manga, NOT superhero dramatic, NOT realistic - friendly accessible comic strip style with vibrant colors. High contrast black outlines on white/light background with colored fills and glows.

CHARACTER DESIGN: Simple but expressive, slightly exaggerated proportions for personality, consistent with existing story illustrations (see ch06-01 reference). All characters recognizable at small sizes.

TECHNICAL SPECS: 600x600px square format, clean line art with flat color fills and strategic glow effects, sRGB color space, optimized for both thumbnail and full-size viewing, title-ready composition.
```
