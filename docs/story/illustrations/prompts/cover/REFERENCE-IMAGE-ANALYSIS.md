### COLOR Addition (Making It Colored vs B&W)

Since story illustrations are black and white but cover needs color:

**Approach:**
- Maintain clean black line art as foundation (2-3px outlines on everything)
- Add flat color fills within the black outlines
- Use strategic glows ONLY where story-appropriate:
  - Cyan (#00d4ff) for monitors, brain architecture, code displays
  - Orchid (#ba55d3) for Mrs. G's ethereal presence (soft glow, particles)
  - Purple (#7b61ff) for Copilot's body panels and circuitry
  - Warm amber/yellow for ambient basement lighting
  - White highlights for dramatic emphasis

**NOT:**
- Full gradient shading (keep it flat color fills)
- Photorealistic lighting and shadows
- Complex texture overlays
- Gradient backgrounds (keep it light and simple)

Think: **Modern webcomic coloring** - clean flats with strategic highlights, NOT painted illustration

## 🚫 Common AI Generation Mistakes to Avoid

Based on reference analysis, explicitly REJECT these if they appear:

1. **Wrong Hair Color:** Brown, black, or dark hair on Mr. Codenstein (MUST be white/gray)
2. **Wrong Robot Design:** Angular intimidating military-style robot (MUST be friendly rounded)
3. **Wrong Robot Face:** Complex detailed LED panel grid (MUST be simple screen with emoticon)
4. **Wrong Art Style:** Photorealistic, manga, superhero comic dramatic (MUST be newspaper comic strip)
5. **Wrong Background Color:** Dark navy or black background (MUST be light cream/off-white)
6. **Wrong Line Weight:** Thin sketchy lines or overly thick manga-style (MUST be consistent 2-3px)
7. **Wrong Shading:** Gradient shading or airbrush effects (MUST be flat colors with strategic cross-hatching)
8. **Wrong Character Proportions:** Overly exaggerated cartoon (MUST be grounded comic strip characters)
