## 🎨 CONCEPT 1: The Basement Laboratory Trinity
**Visual Style:** Cinematic illustration, dramatic lighting, cyberpunk meets homey chaos

**Prompt:**
```
A cinematic book cover illustration for "The Awakening of CORTEX" showing three characters in a chaotic New Jersey basement laboratory. CENTER: Asif Codenstein, a focused developer with wild, unkempt hair, wearing a hoodie, hunched over glowing triple monitors displaying code and neural network diagrams. LEFT SIDE: Mrs. G materializing as a translucent holographic woman with patient, supportive expression, rendered in medium orchid (#ba55d3) ethereal glow. RIGHT SIDE: Copilot, a sleek humanoid robot assistant with purple (#7b61ff) LED accents and glowing eyes, holding a coffee mug. BACKGROUND: Whiteboards covered in diagrams, sticky notes forming neural networks, seventeen coffee mugs scattered everywhere, Christmas lights tangled with cables, storage boxes labeled "critical infrastructure." LIGHTING: Cyan (#00d4ff) monitor glow illuminating Asif's face, warm golden basement lighting, dramatic shadows. MOOD: Intense focus, creative chaos, technological awakening. TITLE SPACE: Top third reserved for "The Awakening of CORTEX" title. Art style: Digital illustration, hyper-detailed, cyberpunk aesthetic with cozy undertones, 600x600px square format.
```

**Why This Works:** Shows all three characters in their natural habitat with story-accurate details (the chaos, the coffee, the basement setting). Centers Asif as protagonist while giving equal visual weight to his AI companions.
