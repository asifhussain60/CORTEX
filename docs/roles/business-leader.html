CORTEX goes beyond AI assistance to agentic development — multi-step, autonomous engineering workflows that plan, implement, test, review, and certify without constant human prompting. This is not a pilot. It is the production architecture.

Agentic Development with CORTEX

290+ orchestrators coordinate autonomously — breaking work into phases, selecting the right specialist for each task, self-verifying results, and escalating only when human judgment is genuinely required. End-to-end feature delivery without per-step prompting.

Generative AI — Orchestrated, Not Raw

CORTEX does not expose raw LLM access to engineering workflows. Every generative AI call is governed, context-enriched with LENS analysis, and validated against governance rules before the output reaches the codebase. Generative capability without generative risk.

Prompt Engineering as Architecture

CORTEX's prompt suite is a governed, version-controlled, self-refreshing asset — not a collection of ad-hoc instructions. Prompts are introspected against the live codebase and regenerated automatically. The organisation's AI behaviour is codified, auditable, and drift-free.

RCA Synthesis — Prevention Over Reaction

The RCA Memory Engine applies four analysis methodologies (Five-Whys, Fishbone, Fault-Tree, Causal-Chain) to every significant failure, then generates an automated prevention rule shared organisation-wide. Root cause analysis becomes a continuous process, not a quarterly retrospective.

Self-Governing Enforcement

60+ governance rules run automatically at every commit, every build, and every runtime operation. No team member audits compliance manually. Standards either enforce themselves or block automatically — no exceptions, no overrides without a recorded reason.

Self-Extending Architecture
