What is CORTEX?

CORTEX is an AI Engineering Orchestration Framework that governs how AI-assisted code is planned, written, tested, and delivered. It wraps GitHub Copilot with a structured lifecycle — enforcing TDD, governance rules, and sweep-complete quality gates — so every output meets a verified standard before it ships.

Intelligent AI Engineering

CORTEX operates as an advanced AI co-pilot. It builds upon GitHub Copilot by introducing structured discipline, architectural awareness, and automated quality standards designed to help software teams scale their delivery reliably.

Functioning seamlessly within your IDE, it analyzes intent, enforces your team's established coding standards, and acts as a sophisticated partner that assists in catching structural issues early in the development lifecycle.

Whether operating as a solo developer or within a large enterprise, CORTEX helps transform standard AI assistance into a governed delivery workflow that evolves alongside your engineering practices.

The CORTEX Vision

Modern engineering teams face challenges maintaining consistent quality under rapid delivery pressures. CORTEX aims to address this by introducing a framework where quality checks are infrastructure, not afterthoughts.

Our vision is to facilitate software delivery with enhanced predictability. By analyzing execution patterns and managing context strategically, CORTEX helps teams build an institutional memory that assists in onboarding and knowledge retention.

As your team interacts with the platform, it continuously adapts, ensuring that your architectural decisions and security postures remain consistently applied across all new implementations.

dogfooding

/ˈdôɡˌfuːdiNG/
noun (software engineering)
The practice of an organization using its own product during development to test and improve it. By "eating your own dog food," creators directly experience the realities, flaws, and capabilities of their software before users do.
