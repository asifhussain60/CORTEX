# Character Consistency Sheet — P0 Canonical Reference

> **Rule:** Every story prompt MUST copy these character blocks verbatim. Character faces, body types, and core visual identity are IMMUTABLE across all images in all chapters. Only outfit, hair style, expression, and wave-color accent change per scene.

## 🧑‍🔬 Asif Codenstein — The ADHD Architect

### Immutable Physical Identity
- **Age/Build:** 54-year-old eccentric mad scientist. Youthful-looking despite his age. Plump — round full cheeks, soft belly, the lovably round silhouette of someone well-fed on the thrill of coding and the neglect of nutritional planning. NOT fat — endearingly, specifically plump. Soft pudgy fingers.
- **Hair:** Wild Einstein-meets-Chaplin white-streaked dark hair. Maximum entropy at all times — gravity is a suggestion, not a rule. Different chaos configuration per image (one tuft up, one sideways, pillow-crease on one side, comet-trail motion lines, etc.) to convey ADHD energy. Hair has its own gravitational field.
- **Glasses:** Thick round glasses, perpetually askew — one arm slightly bent, both lenses at slightly different heights. Occasionally: one lens cracked and taped, steamed from sprinting, pushed up on forehead during sleep.
- **Default Clothing:** Dark hoodie (pulls slightly across the chest), often with a sticker or pin (e.g., "BIG BRAIN TIME", "TDD"). Spider-Man pajamas for all night/sleep scenes. Mismatched footwear (one sneaker + one slide, or mismatched socks). When forced into "real clothes": trousers slightly too short, mismatched socks visible.
- **Signature Props:** Red whiteboard marker (always), "DEBUG FUEL" ceramic mug (always on desk), neon yellow "847. Never again." sticky note on monitor, sticky notes accumulated on person/desk/hair.

### Per-Scene Variables
- Expression, posture, specific hair chaos, specific hoodie sticker, specific pajama state, which sticky notes are stuck where.

## 💜 Miss G — The Imaginary Girlfriend

### Immutable Physical Identity
- **Nature:** Semi-translucent — rendered like frosted starlight. Present, vivid, a full visual character. Features and form are crisp and clear, NOT faded or ghostly. Her outline is bold and solid, matching the ink weight of all other character lines. A warm glow-halo rings her silhouette, emanating from within her translucent form.
- **Outline Color:** Matches chapter wave accent color (purple → cyan → amber → emerald → violet). The glow-halo matches the same accent.

- **Hair:** Naturally curly voluminous black ringlets, below-shoulder length. Different elegant style per image (crown braid, high puff, low ponytail, loose and free, side-swept, etc.) — always curly, always voluminous, always black.

- **Hair Style:** Different arrangement per image. Always curly black ringlets.
- **Outfit — World National Dress Rotation:** Different country's traditional/national dress per image. Always rendered semi-translucent, glowing with the wave accent color. Always gloriously modest.

## 🤖 Copilot Bot (CB) — The Scarecrow Gets a Brain

### Immutable Physical Identity
- **Nature:** Physical chrome-plated robot. Solid, not translucent. LED eyes. Squeaky casters.
- **Core Feature:** Transparent dome on head — the "brain cavity." This is the PRIMARY visual evolution tracker across the entire story.
- **Speech:** ALL-CAPS, enthusiastic, earnest. Speech bubbles in every scene.

### Evolution Arc (IMMUTABLE — tied to chapter progression)

| Phase | Chapters | Dome Contents | Body Proportions | Personality |
|-------|----------|---------------|------------------|-------------|
| **Infant** | 01–04 | Empty transparent cavity. Void. Nothing inside. | Oversized wobbly dome, clunky chassis, disproportionate. Goofy. | Cheerful menace. Zero context. "I SUGGEST WE DELETE THE DATABASE!" energy. |
| **Toddler** | 05–06 | Very faint, sparse floating particle-dots. Barely visible. | Slightly more composed. Still wobbly. | Starting to ask questions instead of just suggesting deletion. |
| **Adolescent** | 07–08 | Sparse but visible network of wave-color glowing dots in a faint pattern. | More proportional. Less top-heavy. | Self-aware about limitations. Experiencing "regret" and "emotions." |
| **Young Adult** | 09–10 | Clearly visible lattice of connected dots. Early network forming. | Notably more measured. Refined proportions. | Purposeful. Efficient. Still earnest but more thoughtful. |
| **Adult** | 11 | Dense, organized neural network. Emerald glow. Beautiful. | Poised, upright, quiet confidence. Chrome reflects environment. | Calm. Makes genuinely insightful suggestions. The 80% threshold insight. |
| **Complete Brain** | 12 | FULL luminous brain filling the entire dome. Radiant. Magnificent. | Iron Man / Vision posture — confident, powerful, elegant. Diploma in hand. | Profound. Philosophical. "Receptionists all the way down." "Failure is the cost of admission." |

### LED Eye Color
- Matches chapter wave accent: purple (01–04) → cyan (05–08) → amber (09–10) → emerald (11) → violet (12)
