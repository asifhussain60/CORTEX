Key Principles

Microservices Fundamentals:
Single Responsibility: Each service handles one business capability
Independence: Deploy, scale, and fail independently
Decentralization: Distributed level1-domain-icon--data management and governance
Resilience: Design for failure with circuit breakers and retries
Observability: Metrics, logs, and distributed tracing
Automation: CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure as code
